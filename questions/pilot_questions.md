## Three development questions

These questions guide the initial data-feasibility investigation and prototype development. They are not a finalized evaluation set.

1. **Question 1 Historical ice event:** A question about a historical ice event, such as the beginning of ice cover

   When was the beginning of permanent ice cover reported at Haapanantien ranta, Torankijärvi, for winter 2024-2025?

- **Required Observation:**

  A record identifying the site, the beginning-of-permanent-ice-cover event, and its date for the requested winter

- **Required Explanatory context:**

  The meaning of the observation category.

  The distinction between an event date and the date a record was submitted, if the source distinguishes them.

  The relationship between the observation site and the lake.

- **What a satisfactory answer should include:**

  The reported date, observation site, and source.

  Wording that presents this as a reported observation.

- **What the system must not conclude:**

  That the record establishes conditions everywhere on the lake.

  That the event date establishes ice thickness or safety.

2. **Question:** A question about measurement, such as the latest reported thickness

   What is the most recent ice-thickness measurement available for Haapanantien ranta, Torankijärvi, and when was it observed?

- **Required Observation:**

  A record explicitly identified as an ice-thickness measurement, including value, unit, observation date and site.

  Availability of such records is not yet confirmed.

- **Required Explanatory context:**

  Documentation distinguishing thickness measurements from other ice-event categories.

  The meaning of the measurement fields and units.

  The scope of the records searched when selecting the latest result.

- **What a satisfactory answer should include:**

  The measurement, unit, observation date, site, and source.

  Clear wording that it is the latest record found within the retrieved data, unless broader coverage has been established.

  If no suitable record is found, an explicit statement of that limit.

- **What the system must not conclude:**

  That an unexplained numeric field is a thickness measurement.

  That an old observation describes current conditions.

  That not finding a measurement means there is no ice.

3. **Question:** A question about the system to recognize insufficient evidence

   Does a record of when permanent ice cover began establish the current ice thickness at this site?

- **Required Observation:**

  A freeze-up event record, with its date and site.

  For this development case, no thickness measurement is supplied.

- **Required Explanatory context:**

  The distinction between an event record and a measurement.

  The distinction between a historical observation and current conditions.

- **What a satisfactory answer should include:**

  An explanation that the supplied record reports an ice-cover event but does not provide a current thickness measurement.

  A statement of what additional evidence would be needed.

- **What the system must not conclude:**

  A numerical thickness not present in the evidence.

  That the absence of a supplied thickness value proves no measurement exists elsewhere.

  Any conclusion about whether the ice is safe.
